A personal practice

Sahan Mackenzie is an interior designer and creative principal working across residential and hospitality spaces in Sri Lanka.

His practice brings together a respect for craft, restraint in composition and an instinct for spaces that become more meaningful with use. Biography, credentials and project history to be written from Sahan’s approved founder input.

This site represents Sahan personally. It does not claim a studio, company or collaborative team until those details are formally established.
