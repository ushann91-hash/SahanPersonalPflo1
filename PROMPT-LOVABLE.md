# Lovable Prompt — Sahan Mackenzie Personal Portfolio

Create a polished, responsive personal portfolio website for **Sahan Mackenzie**, an **Interior Designer and Creative Principal** based in Sri Lanka. This is a personal brand site, not a studio or venture website. Keep it calm, editorial, warm and highly considered: quiet luxury through restraint, proportion, generous whitespace and outstanding project photography. Do not copy any other designer’s website, text, name, logo or layout.

## Visual direction

- Palette: paper ivory `#F3F0E8`, deep charcoal-green `#1D211C`, moss `#606B52`, restrained clay `#B98C70`.
- Typography: elegant editorial serif for headlines; restrained clean sans-serif and a tiny monospaced detail font for labels.
- Use a small “SM” monogram only as a temporary placeholder and a wordmark reading “Sahan Mackenzie”.
- Motion should be minimal and slow: gentle image reveal / hover, no scroll-jacking or visual noise.
- Use the supplied rights-cleared images only. Until assets are supplied, use clear non-public placeholders; do not invent photos of completed projects.

## Navigation and pages

Create these pages: Home, Selected Work, Project Detail template, Profile, Services and Start a Conversation.

**Home:** hero statement “Interiors with a quiet sense of place.”; short positioning; signature image; approach section; selected-work preview; dark enquiry band.

**Selected Work:** a spacious, image-led grid for 3–5 verified projects. Names/locations/credits must remain placeholders until confirmed.

**Project Detail:** hero image, concise brief, response, factual project-details table, image gallery and full collaborator/photo credits. Include a clear “rights-cleared source required” content state while assets are incomplete.

**Profile:** personal practice story, founder portrait and three principles. Do not claim Sahan owns a studio/company, has staff, awards or a team unless supplied as approved facts.

**Services:** Interior Direction; Spatial & Interior Design; Furniture, Fixtures & Equipment; Living Systems; Project Collaboration. **Living Systems** is a design-led approach to outdoor living, climate-responsive choices, energy and water readiness, delivered with qualified landscape, solar, engineering and home-technology specialists. It must never imply that Sahan personally supplies/installs solar, provides electrical or engineering design, or builds landscapes. Label the scope “to be confirmed before launch”; avoid procurement or construction management claims.

**Start a Conversation:** a short qualifying form for name, email, project type and brief. Do not enable data sending until an approved secure destination, privacy wording and inbox are provided.

## Functional requirements

- Mobile-first, accessible navigation and semantic HTML.
- High-contrast, keyboard-friendly interactions and visible focus states.
- Optimised responsive images, descriptive alt text, page titles and meta descriptions.
- Keep content in a simple editable data structure so new projects can be added easily.
- Do not connect external services, publish the site, change existing GitHub repositories, or create a custom domain without approval.

Start by reproducing the local prototype’s information architecture and visual restraint, then await approved copy and imagery before treating it as launch-ready.
