## Content rules

- Never publish a client’s home location, budget, plan or imagery without explicit permission.
- Credit photographers, architects, contractors, makers and collaborators accurately.
- Do not use “award-winning”, “full-service studio”, procurement or build-management language unless evidence and operational responsibility exist.
- Keep personal work separate from the later venture brand.

## Emerging service: Living Systems

**Living Systems** is the approved working name for a future-facing, design-led service connecting outdoor living, climate-responsive interiors, energy and water readiness, and home technology. It is not a substitute name for solar installation.

Before marketing it as available, confirm the partner roster, referral/commercial arrangement, responsibilities, insurance, hand-off process and the precise language for landscape, solar, engineering and technology work. Public wording must make clear that Sahan leads the spatial and design integration while qualified specialists deliver regulated design, supply, installation and construction.

## Assets to commission or curate

1. One calm founder portrait, natural light, not a corporate headshot.
2. 12–18 rights-cleared project images per featured project: establishing, wide, material, detail and lived-in moments.
3. Simple portrait-oriented material studies for breaks between case studies.
4. Final wordmark/monogram as editable SVG, plus PNG fallback and favicon.
5. A photo-credit sheet containing creator, licence, approval contact, usage and expiry.
