# Editorial Content

Write in a quiet, observant and specific voice. Use tangible language such as light, material, rhythm, joinery, threshold, scale and daily use. Avoid generic luxury language: “timeless”, “world-class”, “bespoke” and “transformative”.

- Never invent biography, awards, client names, dates, locations, budgets, collaborators or service scope.
- Home: 6–12 word statement plus two sentences that explain Sahan’s perspective.
- Profile: 120–220 words from founder-approved facts.
- Services: clear outcome, 40–70 word explanation and explicit scope limit.
- Refer to sustainability, gardens, solar, water or technology under **Living Systems** only when the specialist-collaboration model is confirmed. Never imply that Sahan provides regulated engineering, electrical, installation or construction services.
- Case study: verified essentials, brief, design response, atmosphere and credits.
- Journal: publish only when there are 3–5 useful articles. Suitable subjects include material notes, briefing an interior designer, heritage context, process and makers’ craft.

Every blog/article draft must include title, standfirst, author, date, category, featured-image credit and practical takeaway. Mark anything awaiting founder approval.
