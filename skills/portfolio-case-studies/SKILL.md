# Portfolio Case Studies

Launch with three to five strong, rights-cleared projects rather than a large, uneven archive.

For every project, confirm public name, client permission, Sahan’s exact role, scope, broad location permission, year, collaborators, photographer, image licence, required credit and asset expiry. Do not publish private homes, plans, budgets or images without documented approval.

Use this structure: 1) essentials, 2) brief, 3) response with concrete choices, 4) atmosphere grounded in actual material/light/use, 5) credits.

Use real project photography only. Do not represent completed work with AI-generated interiors. If documentation or permission is incomplete, keep it out of the public portfolio.
