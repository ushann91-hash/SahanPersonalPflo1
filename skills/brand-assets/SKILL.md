# Brand Assets

Maintain a restrained personal identity: Sahan Mackenzie wordmark, optional SM monogram, warm paper ground, charcoal-green ink, moss and clay accents. Let real work lead the identity.

- Treat AI-generated logo marks as concepts only, never final legal or production assets.
- Before public use, select/refine with Sahan, redraw the chosen mark as editable SVG, and run an appropriate similarity/trademark check.
- Maintain raster fallbacks, favicon, clear-space guidance and colour variants after approval.
- Curate photographic sequences: establishing view, spatial view, material, detail and lived-in use. Record photographer credit and permissions.
- Never create fictitious interior photographs to stand in for Sahan’s projects.

When handing off assets, include filename, owner/source, usage permission, credit text, dimensions and format.
